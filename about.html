✦ Our Story ✦

Born from a love of nature

Leish Essentials started as a passion project — a desire to create clean, natural products that feel luxurious. Every candle poured, every soap crafted and every gloss made is a little piece of us, made just for you.

Why Leish Essentials?

We believe that what you put on your body matters just as much as what you put in it. That's why every single product in our range is made with natural ingredients. Just pure, honest goodness crafted with intention. 🌿

From our hand-poured candles to our nourishing soaps and glossy lip treats — every product is a labour of love. We're not just selling products, we're sharing a lifestyle rooted in self-care, nature and intentional living.

Our Promise to You

In accordance with the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA), we are committed to being fully transparent and fair in everything we do. As our customer, you have the right to:

  • ✅ Receive goods that are safe, of good quality and fit for purpose
  • ✅ Be honestly informed about ingredients, pricing and product details
  • ✅ Return or exchange items that are defective or not as described
  • ✅ Have your personal information handled with care and respect

If you ever have a concern, question or complaint — please reach out. We genuinely care about your experience. We will respond to all queries within 2–3 business days.
