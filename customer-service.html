💛 Looking after your Leish Essentials products properly ensures you get the most out of every single one. Here's everything you need to know!

🕯️ Candle Care

  • First Burn: Always allow your candle to burn until the entire top layer of wax has melted on the first use. This prevents tunnelling and ensures an even burn every time.
  • Burn Time: Never burn your candle for more than 4 hours at a time. Allow it to cool and resolidify before relighting.
  • Wick Trimming: Always trim your wick to 5mm before each burn. This prevents black soot, mushrooming and uneven flames.
  • Safety: Never leave a burning candle unattended. Keep away from children, pets, drafts and flammable materials.
  • Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to prevent discolouration or melting.
  • Last Burn: Stop burning when 1cm of wax remains at the bottom to protect the container from heat damage.

🧴 Body Butter Care

  • Application: Apply to clean, slightly damp skin for best absorption. A little goes a long way!
  • Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Do not store in a hot bathroom or near a window.
  • Temperature: Body butters are natural and will soften in heat and firm up in cold — this is completely normal and does not affect quality.
  • Hygiene: Always use clean, dry hands or a spatula when scooping your butter to prevent contamination.
  • Shelf Life: Best used within 6–12 months of purchase.

💆 Body Oil & Hair Oil Care

  • Body Oil: Apply to clean, damp skin after showering for maximum absorption and a lasting glow.
  • Hair Oil: Apply a small amount to the ends of hair or scalp. Avoid over-applying to roots to prevent greasiness.
  • Storage: Keep bottles tightly closed and store away from direct sunlight and extreme heat.
  • Shelf Life: Best used within 12 months of purchase.
  • Patch Test: If you have sensitive skin, always do a patch test before full application.

🧼 Soap Care

  • Keep it Dry: Always allow your soap to dry completely between uses. Use a well-draining soap dish to prevent it from sitting in water.
  • Storage: Store unused soaps in a cool, dry place. Avoid humid environments.
  • Appearance: Natural soaps may develop a white haze called "soap bloom" — this is completely normal and does not affect quality. Simply rinse it off.
  • Shelf Life: Best used within 12 months of purchase.

💄 Lip Gloss Care

  • Hygiene: Never share your lip gloss with others to prevent the spread of bacteria or infection.
  • Application: Use the provided applicator only. Avoid touching the applicator directly to your lips if possible — apply and then press lips together.
  • Storage: Store upright in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Do not leave in a hot car or near a heat source.
  • Shelf Life: Best used within 6–12 months of opening.

🌀 Resin Product Care

  • Sunlight: Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight as this can cause yellowing or fading of the resin over time.
  • Heat: Do not place resin items near direct heat sources (e.g. stoves, heaters, car dashboards in summer) as resin can warp or soften.
  • Cleaning: Wipe gently with a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cloths.
  • Handling: Although resin is durable, avoid dropping your pieces as they can chip or crack on hard surfaces.

🖊️ Printed Items Care (Cups, Mugs & Shirts)

  • Cups & Mugs: Hand wash only — do not put in the dishwasher as this can cause the design to peel or fade over time.
  • Shirts: Wash inside out on a gentle, cold cycle. Do not tumble dry or iron directly over the printed design.
  • General: Avoid soaking printed items for extended periods and do not use bleach or harsh detergents on the printed areas.

✨ The Artisanal Difference

At Leish Essentials, every product — from our candles and soaps to our body butters, body oils, hair oils, lip glosses, and resin products — is handcrafted in small batches. Because we use natural ingredients and manual techniques, please note:

  • Appearance: Slight variations in colour, shape, or botanical placement are normal and make your product unique.
  • Texture & Consistency: Hand-whipped butters, oils, and soaps may react to temperature changes during transit (e.g., softening in heat or slight cloudiness in cold). This does not affect the quality or safety of the product.
  • Scent: Small variations in scent intensity can occur between batches due to the nature of organic essential oils and fragrances.
  • Resin Products: Each resin piece is individually handmade. Slight variations in colour, finish, or design are expected and are part of what makes each piece one of a kind.

These characteristics are a guarantee of a truly handmade product, not a defect.

Leish Essentials uses third-party courier services. While we pack every order with maximum protection, we are not liable for delays or mishandling caused by the courier.

How to Start a Return

Return Shipping & Refunds

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer. If our inspection confirms a manufacturing defect, we will refund the shipping cost and the item value. If the defect is found to be falsified or caused by customer mishandling, no refund will be issued.

Refunds are processed via EFT within 5–7 business days of inspection. Original shipping fees are non-refundable.

Our returns policy is in accordance with the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA). Note that the CPA does not mandate returns for "change of mind" on handcrafted health/beauty goods or items tailored to the consumer.
